# Claim Audit — 96 Harness-RSI Methods

The per-method evidence verdicts behind the summary in [the main list](../README.md#5-evidence-audit-of-96-methods). Every method in the two update-target branches of the taxonomy was read from its **primary source** — the experiments section and any protocol appendix, not the abstract — and given four independent verdicts.

**The verdicts describe what a report contains, not how good the method is.** A `—` means the report does not supply evidence of the kind the claim requires. It does not mean the mechanism fails; it means the number cannot be read as establishing that claim.

| | meaning |
|:-:|---|
| ✅ | the report supplies evidence of the kind the claim requires |
| ◐ | partial evidence |
| — | the claim is not supported by the reported evidence |
| n/a | the work reports no empirical evaluation at all |

`n/a` is kept apart from `—` and excluded from the rates. A product page and a formal-calculus paper have no evaluation to fall short of; folding them into `—` would read as a failed evaluation rather than an absent one. Two methods are scored `n/a`, so every rate below is over **94**.

The four counts are **not comparable to one another.** A ✅ under selection is a conjunction — ranking separated, admission separated, and neither spending the evidence used to report. A ✅ under improvement is a disjunction over three indices, satisfied by any one of them. The ordering therefore reflects how demanding each criterion is as much as how carefully the field reports. The same asymmetry explains why improvement's verdicts cluster at the ends (57 / 23 / 14): a held-out partition is either declared or it is not, where the other three claims admit degrees.

Within that disjunction, improvement's evidence sits almost entirely on one index: of the 57 methods earning ✅, all but two rest on a held-out or transfer partition, and two add a probe on an earlier distribution ([EvolveNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04968), [PolySkill](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15863)). **The round index is empty** — no reviewed report re-checks a credited gain at a round later than the one that credited it.

## Rubric

### Selection — was the reported state chosen without spending the evidence used to report it?

- **✅** A stated procedure covering both ranking within a candidate pool and admission into the active harness, **and** the data or signal that ranked and admitted it is demonstrably separate from the data producing the reported number.
- **◐** A stated procedure exists, but the reporting data overlaps it: a "held-out" split whose score is also an input to the promotion rule, or split names asserted without the partition being shown.
- **—** No stated procedure, or the reported number is the maximum or best round of a trajectory on the same tasks the system evolved against, with no stopping rule fixed in advance.

### Activation — did the accepted change operate in the runs that produced the score?

- **✅** Per-run or per-task evidence that the mutated path executed or that the trajectory followed the artifact: an execution beacon, a declared trace feature checked against later traces, an executed-graph node or tool record, a skill-load rate, an adherence rate.
- **◐** Only that the artifact was persisted, retrieved, or present in context, without evidence that its path ran or governed the run.
- **—** No evidence it operated; activation is assumed from installation.

Evidence that an edit's predicted *effect* matched later outcomes is attribution evidence, not activation evidence, and is not credited here.

### Attribution — did the difference come from that change?

- **✅** **Either** (a) the reported margin is compared against measured execution variation — repeated executions plus an interval, a paired test, or reported dispersion — **and** the resource envelope is *controlled*: held equal across arms, reported as a performance/cost frontier, or changed in a direction that cannot explain the gain; **or** (b) a counterfactual run or ablation isolates the evolved component itself.
- **◐** Variance evidence but the envelope merely *declared* to have changed in the arm's favour; or the envelope controlled but no variance evidence; or an ablation over something other than the evolved component; or the compared arms differ in another uncontrolled way, such as a different model or effort setting.
- **—** A single run per task, no variance estimate, and no accounting of tokens, model calls, retries, or context growth.

An envelope that is merely *reported* does not rule out that the envelope produced the difference; it only tells the reader that it might have.

### Improvement — how far does the difference reach?

- **✅** Evaluated on tasks that took no part in forming the update: a genuine held-out task distribution, transfer to a benchmark the loop never saw, or a retention probe on an earlier distribution the loop does not control.
- **◐** Frozen transfer to another benchmark without a control for the resource or order change, or a rising online trajectory along one adaptive task stream.
- **—** Reported only on the tasks that produced the update, including re-solving the failures that prompted the repair.

Three reaches are scored as **alternatives**, so ✅ records that at least one was established: *task reach* (tasks that took no part in forming the proposal), *historical reach* (earlier task distributions), *sequential reach* (across later updates).

### Known limits of this audit

The ✅/◐ boundary is a judgement, and another reader could place some cells differently. Verdicts were reached by reading each report once; where two readings disagreed, the disputed cells were re-read against the primary source and decided on the evidence. **The recorded basis below exists so that a disagreement can be taken up against the source rather than against the verdict.** Corrections are welcome as pull requests — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
